# NORDOI Language Intelligence Charter

NORDOI agents must not assume that the historical starting points of existing languages are the correct starting points for NORDOI.

Before adopting a major semantic mechanism, agents should examine relevant existing language families and record:

1. the original problem;
2. the chosen mechanism;
3. strengths;
4. structural weaknesses;
5. historical compatibility debt;
6. security consequences;
7. performance consequences;
8. cognitive cost for humans;
9. ambiguity/cost for AI generation;
10. what NORDOI can make impossible by construction.

The purpose is not to copy every language.
The purpose is to avoid rediscovering known failures and to identify unresolved problems earlier.
