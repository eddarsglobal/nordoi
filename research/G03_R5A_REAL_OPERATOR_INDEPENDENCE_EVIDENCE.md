## Minimal external evidence intake contract (no secrets)

A prospective candidate may provide references to the following **without** submitting raw documents or private keys into this public repository:

- `publisher_controller` must be `eddarsglobal`, not an attacker-supplied different project name.
- `operator_controller` and `witness_controller` must be *different declared* controllers, neither equal to the publisher.
- `operator_identity_evidence_sha256`: digest of identity/custody evidence held privately by an independent reviewer.
- `candidate_public_key_sha256`: digest of the proposed public signing key.
- `r4z_request_binding_sha256`: reference to a particular R4Z challenge, not proof of execution or one-time consumption.
- `owner_oob_receipt_sha256`: claimed digest of owner-approved **out-of-band** enrollment, not authenticated merely by its presence.
- `protected_witness_head_sha256`, `witness_epoch`: untrusted reference and claimed monotonic version, not a protected live high-water.
- `revocation_policy_sha256` and `recovery_policy_sha256`: digests of future policy documents that must be independently reviewed.

The software validates UTF-8 JSON, exact version/project/R4Z parent commit, exact field set, controller identifier grammar, collisions, digest notation, invalid booleans and untrusted epoch floor. It denies symlinks, nonregular files (including FIFO), oversized input and duplicate keys; reads files only and emits only a digest and **BLOCK / NOT_AUTHORIZED**, exit status **2**. Sensitive proof content is **not** stored or forwarded. Even all-green syntactically consistent claims return `INDEPENDENT_CUSTODY_NOT_EXTERNALLY_AUTHENTICATED`. This is deliberate.
