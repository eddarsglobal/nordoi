# Constitutional Conformance & Future-Native Governance Intelligence — G0.1

## Research question

How can NORDOI preserve a decades-scale future-oriented mission without confusing a large number of already-certified low-level invariants with completion of the universal language architecture?

## Decision

Use two independent measures. Constitutional evidence coverage records whether each written principle has evidence in its current explicit scope. Full-delivery coverage uses a transparent weighted planning model across major architecture families. Neither may substitute for the other.

## Anti-feature-parity rule

A capability proposal is not justified merely because another language or operating system exposes it. Every major proposal must identify the constitutional problem, simplify the human model where possible, preserve/improve security and provability, remain backend-independent at the semantic core, and respect frozen certified boundaries.

## Why this is NORDOI-native

The governance layer turns the Constitution into an executable inspection surface. It does not grant authority or execute applications. It gives humans and future AI/SI agents one canonical snapshot of what is proven, what is partial, and where high-value future work remains.

## Evidence discipline

The current p2.7 baseline is immutable. G0.1 consumes that evidence additively and does not reinterpret certified runtime meaning. Planning percentages are explicitly non-certification estimates.
